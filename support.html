Music Tower Stacker

Something wrong, or an idea for a song? Email support@ovsupplies.com.au and put "Music Tower Stacker" in the subject. We read everything.

Common questions

How do I unlock more songs? Finish the song you are playing. Each song you finish opens the next one on the list.

What do coins do? They unlock block colours, backdrops and instrument sounds. They never make the game easier, and they are earned by playing, not bought.

I lost my progress. Progress is stored on the device, so deleting the app clears it. We cannot restore it, because we never had a copy.
