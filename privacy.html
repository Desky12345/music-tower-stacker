Privacy Policy

Music Tower Stacker · last updated 19 September 2026

Music Tower Stacker does not collect any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising in the app.

What the app stores

Your progress is kept on your device only: your best height, your best run of perfect drops, which songs you have finished, your coin balance, what you own, and your settings. None of it leaves your phone, and none of it is sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Purchases

There are none. The app is free and contains no in-app purchases, so no payment details of any kind pass through it.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
