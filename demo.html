Demo recording

Music Tower Stacker 1.0 · captured on iPhone, iOS 26

The recording begins with the app launching from the home screen and shows the whole user flow: a run of thirty-one blocks, the end-of-run card, the song list, all three shop tabs, and the settings screen.

There is no account, no login and no account deletion flow, because the app has no accounts. There is no user-generated content. There are no in-app purchases in this version, so there is no paid content to show.
