Music Tower Stacker

One block, one note. Finish the tune.

Tap to drop a sliding slab onto the tower. Every block you place sounds the next note of the song, so the tune is built by the tower itself. Finish the tune to clear it and open the next one. Every song is out of copyright, and more are added over time.

Coins

Coins are picked up off the tower as you climb. They buy block colours, backdrops and instrument sounds, and nothing else. Nothing you buy makes the game easier or changes your score. Coins can also be bought in the app, and doing so is entirely optional.
